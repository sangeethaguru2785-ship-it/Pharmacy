Our Story

A Modern Pharmacy Built on Trust & Care

Stackly began with a simple mission: to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone. What started as a single neighbourhood pharmacy has grown into one of the most trusted online medicine delivery platforms in the country.

Every order is verified by licensed pharmacists, sourced from certified manufacturers, and delivered with care. We combine the warmth of a local pharmacy with the convenience of modern technology.

Our Journey

Milestones That Made Us

Fourteen years of growth, guided by one simple promise — your health comes first.

2012

First Store Opened

A single neighbourhood pharmacy opens its doors with three employees and one mission — honest healthcare.

2016

Went Online

Stackly.com launches, bringing online ordering and prescription uploads to customers across the city.

2019

Express Delivery Network

30-minute delivery hubs roll out in metro areas, powered by our own fleet of riders.

2022

500,000 Orders Milestone

Half a million verified orders delivered, and our licensed pharmacist helpline goes live around the clock.

2025

Nationwide Expansion

18+ cities, 450 partner pharmacies and 50,000+ families relying on us every single month.
